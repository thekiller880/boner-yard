The Theropoda — warm-blooded, feathered, socially complex. They did not think like lizards. They thought like ravens. High encephalization relative to body mass. Binocular vision. Pack coordination. Tool-adjacent behavior in certain lineages. The peak of their cognitive arc was never witnessed — it ended before it could be recorded by anyone who survived.
Scientific Substrate
Troodon formosus had the highest encephalization quotient of any known dinosaur — comparable to modern emus. Theropod brain endocasts show enlarged cerebral hemispheres and optic lobes consistent with complex processing. Birds ARE theropods; the lineage never ended, it transformed.
